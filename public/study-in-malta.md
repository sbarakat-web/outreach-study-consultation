# Study in Malta with Outreach

Canonical source: https://consultation.outreachstudy.eu/

Last reviewed: 23 October 2026

Outreach Recruitment Agency helps international students compare programmes, prepare an application and book a free Study in Malta consultation. This guide summarizes the public information available on the Outreach consultation website.

Important: prices, scholarships, course availability, intakes, entry requirements and payment schedules are indicative and must be confirmed with Outreach and the institution before enrolment. Institutions make admission decisions. The relevant Maltese authorities make visa and immigration decisions.

## Services

- Compare English courses, diplomas, bachelor's degrees and master's degrees.
- Review indicative tuition fees, duration, qualification level and entry guidance.
- Get help preparing the application-document set.
- Save questions for the consultation interviewer.
- Book a free consultation to identify the most suitable programme.
- Follow application progress through a private candidate dashboard.

Providers shown are London School of Commerce Malta, English Path Language Schools and Kensington English. Displayed examples range from €160 per week for long-duration English Path study, from €1,450 for an 11-week London School of Commerce Malta course, and from £390 for listed Kensington group options. Exact totals, dates, timetables, registration fees and visa suitability must be confirmed.

## Application documents

The candidate portal requests:

1. Updated CV in Europass format
2. Passport bio page
3. Certificates with transcripts
4. IELTS certification, if available
5. A 300-word personal statement

An institution may request additional documents.

## Typical application journey

1. Prepare and upload readable application documents.
2. The chosen institution reviews eligibility and may request an interview or more evidence.
3. Follow the institution's instructions to reserve a place and receive an offer.
4. Accept the offer and pay tuition according to the official schedule.
5. Receive and verify the electronic Letter of Acceptance after the required payment is confirmed.
6. Use the latest official checklist and submit the visa application through the appropriate channel for the country of residence.

Official checklist: https://identita.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/03a.-List-of-Required-Documents-Student_National_Visa.pdf

## Frequently asked questions

### What can I study in Malta?

The catalogue covers English-language courses plus programmes in business, accountancy and finance, information technology, health and social care, tourism and hospitality, and logistics.

### How much does it cost to study in Malta?

Tuition varies by institution, programme and duration. The public catalogue displays indicative fees, while Outreach and the institution confirm the final fee and payment schedule before enrolment.

### Is IELTS required?

English requirements vary. Some programmes may accept an institutional assessment or other evidence; others specify IELTS or an equivalent qualification.

### Can Outreach guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Outreach provides programme, document and process guidance. Institutions decide admission, and the relevant Maltese authorities decide visa and immigration applications.
